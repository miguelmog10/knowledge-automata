# DEC-0007: Launch count screen and status picker in English and French

Back to the [decision log](../decision-log.md).

## Decision

Launch the count screen and the status picker in English and French.

## Context

Two French-speaking growers require the count screen and status picker in French.

## Rationale

To meet the two French-speaking growers' need for those screens in French.

## Alternatives considered

- Translating every screen
- Launching in English only

## Decided by

Marc Gagnon

## Decided on

2026-06-09

## Impacts

The count screen and status picker will be available in both English and French at launch.
