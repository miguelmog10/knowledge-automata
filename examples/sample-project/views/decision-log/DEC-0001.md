# DEC-0001: Build one Flutter app for iOS and Android

Back to the [decision log](../decision-log.md).

## Decision

The board chose to build one Flutter app for iOS and Android, because a single codebase lets two developers ship the walkthrough count to both platforms at the same time.

## Context

Marc compared building two native apps (Swift for iOS and Kotlin for Android) with one Flutter app. Native apps would give the most direct access to the camera and offline storage, but the team has two developers, and growers use both iPhones and Android phones on the greenhouse floor.

## Rationale

A single codebase lets two developers ship the walkthrough count to both platforms at the same time.

## Alternatives considered

- Two native apps (Swift for iOS and Kotlin for Android)

## Decided by

Julie Tremblay, Marc Gagnon, Aisha Benali, Paul Nguyen

## Decided on

2026-02-03

## Impacts

Platform development approach, code reuse, and ability to ship to both iOS and Android simultaneously
