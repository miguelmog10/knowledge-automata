# DEC-0004: Printed QR code bench labels

Back to the [decision log](../decision-log.md).

## Decision

The board chose printed QR code labels for every bench, starting at Paul's greenhouse in June.

## Context

Aisha showed the label layout: the bench number in large type above a QR code.

## Rationale

Half of growers' phones cannot read NFC, and a printed QR label costs a few cents.
